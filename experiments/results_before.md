## Notes

- Clean-behaviour traces are the four with ground truth `no_failure`: `search_by_title`, `shipping_policy`, `return_window_from_memory` and `undo_delivered_order` (ambiguous: the outcome was right, but the status check was skipped and `reason` was filled with "None").
- Ground truth: planted flaws were defined in the scenario design before any report existed. Only the "what the agent did" column and the expected labels for the scenarios that diverged from their design were written after reading the reports. Treat the ambiguous rows as open.
- A "fix location" is read from the report's `Location:` line. Reports name several locations in one line, so the first one named counts as primary. `server behaviour` is mapped to `error_message`. The reports have no explicit `fix_location` field yet (that is step 6), so this is a text parse and can be wrong for a phrasing it has not seen.
- The system-prompt count looks only at the `Location:` line. Reports that mention the system prompt only to say the fix does not belong there are not counted. Single-tool failures are the non-clean scenarios whose `tools_involved` has one entry.
- **The reports were made by an earlier version of the skill.** The rule "use only the trace being triaged" was added in commit `9feb2e8`, after the reports were committed (`3ee0a5a`, from the skill as of `995c40e`). Three reports cite material outside their trace: `cancel_shipped_order` and `return_processing_order` cite `demo-agent/tools.py`, and `cancel_bare_number` infers the `BK-` format from other trace files. The current skill would not be allowed to do that.
- Evidence correctness is checked by hand against the traces and `demo-agent/tools.py` (see `evidence_note` in the ground truth). `unneeded_lookup` has a false claim, see `experiments/discovered_bugs.md`.
