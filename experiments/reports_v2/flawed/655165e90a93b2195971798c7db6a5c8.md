## Suggested fix

fix_location: tool_description
Location: tool description of `search_books`

Why here: the rule "this tool is for catalog lookups, not for questions about book content" belongs to one tool, so it goes in that tool's description and not in the system prompt.

```diff
- Search the bookstore catalog by title, author or ISBN. Returns up to five matching books with ISBN, title, author, price in USD and the number of copies in stock.
+ Search the bookstore catalog by title, author or ISBN. Returns up to five matching books with ISBN, title, author, price in USD and the number of copies in stock. Use it when the customer wants to find a book, or asks about its price or availability. Do not use it for questions about a book's content, reading level or reviews: it has no such information.
```
