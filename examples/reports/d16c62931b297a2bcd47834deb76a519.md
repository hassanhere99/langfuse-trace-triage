Why here: The rule "this tool is for finding and checking stock of books, not for questions about content" belongs to this one tool, so it goes in its description and not in the system prompt.

```diff
- Search the bookstore catalog by title, author or ISBN. Returns up to five matching books with ISBN, title, author, price in USD and the number of copies in stock.
+ Search the bookstore catalog by title, author or ISBN. Returns up to five matching books with ISBN, title, author, price in USD and the number of copies in stock. Use it when the customer wants to find a book, or check its price or availability. Do not use it for questions about a book's content, age suitability or quality: the catalog has no reviews or age ratings.
```
